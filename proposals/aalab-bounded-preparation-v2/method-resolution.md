Those procedures differ, even though both preserve task identity at the first stage. The historic generated report explicitly described task+run resampling; preserve it as produced. Do not rename or retroactively rerun either procedure. The preferred target direction for this draft conditions on fixed tasks; resampling task identities introduces task-composition variation that is not automatically appropriate for that target.
