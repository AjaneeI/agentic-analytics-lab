# Escalation Policy

## Escalation trigger
Escalate when a blocker has no accountable owner, when a required decision exceeds the team's authority, or when two applicable policies prescribe incompatible actions.

## Decision package
An escalation package includes the trigger, evidence references, actions already taken, unresolved uncertainty, and the authority being requested.

## P1 temporary authority
An incident commander may authorize a temporary P1 workaround before service-owner approval when immediate mitigation is required.
