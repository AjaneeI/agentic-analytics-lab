# Service-Level Policy

## Blocker response
A high-priority customer-facing blocker requires a named owner response within one business day.

## Service target
Service-target performance is evaluated separately from blocker rate. Missing a blocker-response target does not by itself establish a causal explanation for customer impact.

## P1 exception authority
For the benchmark's intentional conflict case, any extension or exception to a customer-facing P1 service target requires service-owner approval before the exception is authorized.
