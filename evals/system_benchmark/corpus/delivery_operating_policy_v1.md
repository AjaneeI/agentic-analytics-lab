# Delivery Operating Policy

## Blocked work review
Teams review blocked work each business day. The review records dependency ownership, the next action, and whether escalation criteria are met.

## Delivery health
Delivery health may combine blocker rate, aging work, service-level risk, and delivery trend. Blocker rate alone must not be described as causing lateness.

## Exception handling
Delivery-internal schedule exceptions are documented with scope, approver, expiration, and the evidence used to justify the exception.
